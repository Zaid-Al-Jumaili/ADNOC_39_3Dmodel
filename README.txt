Level 37 - existing floor - 3D model
====================================

A single web page with the 3D model of the EXISTING Level 37 floor (as-built + site survey), nothing else.

To open: double-click Start_Existing_3D_Model.bat. Your browser opens at http://localhost:8761/ (Chrome or Edge recommended).
Keep the small black window open while viewing; close it to stop. Nothing is installed and nothing is sent to the internet.

Controls: drag to orbit - scroll to zoom - click a zone label to fly there. Right-drag (or two fingers) pans.

The model and its baked lighting are the "Existing" model of the AL MASAR design experience (16 Oct 2026).
